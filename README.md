# National Road Safety & Accident Cost Intelligence System

A government-grade analytics platform built using official Indian road accident datasets to analyze accident risk zones, economic loss and forecast future accident trends.

## Tech Stack
Python (Pandas, SciPy, Prophet), Power BI, Government of India Datasets

## Modules
• Data Cleaning & Engineering  
• Risk Index Modeling  
• Economic Loss Analytics  
• Statistical Hypothesis Testing  
• Accident Forecasting (Prophet)  
• National Power BI Dashboards  

## Dashboards
1. National Safety Overview  
2. State Risk Heatmap  
3. Economic Loss Analytics  
4. Vehicle Risk Intelligence  
5. Trend & Forecast Panel  
6. Policy Action & Funding Priority Panel  

## Impact
Identifies high-risk states, major loss drivers and predicts future accident trends to support proactive government policy planning.
